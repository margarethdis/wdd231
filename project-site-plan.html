Hands and Notes

Individual Project Site Plan - WDD 231

Site Name

Hands and Notes

The name represents the two things a beginner needs to learn: the hand positions on the piano and the notes on the staff. It is short and easy to remember.

Optional domain: handsandnotes.org

Site Purpose

This site teaches beginners with no previous musical experience how to read music and play basic hymns on the piano. It has three main sections:

  1. An introduction to the purpose and benefits of learning to read music.
  2. A guide to music reading fundamentals: the staff, notes, rhythm, and note placement on the keyboard.
  3. A section with recommended hymns, resources, and practice tips for beginners.

I chose this topic because my congregation needs more pianists, and a well-structured resource can help people who want to learn but don't know where to start.

Scenarios

  • How can I learn to recognize the notes on the staff if I have never played an instrument before?
  • Where can I find step-by-step instructions to play a simple hymn with both hands?
  • What basic chords do I need to know to accompany the most common hymns?
